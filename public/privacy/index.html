Privacy

Last updated: September 19, 2026.

The checker does not upload your CSV

DedupeSafe parses and compares contacts inside your browser. The checker has no account system, database, analytics script or contact-data API. Its Content Security Policy blocks runtime network connections with connect-src 'none'.

What the hosting provider can record

Like any hosted website, Vercel can record ordinary requests for pages and static files. Those request logs may include the requested path, time, browser information and network address. DedupeSafe does not send your CSV contents, contact count, mapping, match results or export decisions with those requests.

Downloads

The cleaned CSV and audit report are created in browser memory and downloaded directly to your device.

Support

Public feedback submitted through GitHub Issues is visible to everyone. Do not attach contact exports or personal data to an issue.
