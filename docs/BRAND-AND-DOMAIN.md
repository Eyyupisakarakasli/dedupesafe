# Brand and domain decision

Date: 2026-09-19

## Decision

The independent product name is **DedupeSafe**. HubSpot appears only in
referential copy such as “for HubSpot CSV exports.” The product does not use a
HubSpot logo, icon or trade dress.

The production hostname for the first release is
`https://dedupesafe.vercel.app`. A paid custom domain will not be purchased
before real demand validation. If the product earns repeat use, the preferred
domain is `dedupesafe.app`, subject to availability and a trademark review at
the time of purchase.

Every public surface must include this statement:

> HubSpot is a trademark of HubSpot, Inc. DedupeSafe is independent of HubSpot,
> Inc. and is not authorized, endorsed, sponsored, affiliated with or otherwise
> approved by HubSpot, Inc.

This naming decision reduces confusion. It is not a trademark registration or
legal opinion.
