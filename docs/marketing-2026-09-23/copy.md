## Consultant / agency invitation

Title: Review a HubSpot contact export before connecting another app

I built DedupeSafe for reviewing a client's contact CSV before making changes to their portal.

The checker processes the file in your browser. You don't connect HubSpot or create a DedupeSafe account. It suggests candidate groups; you choose the source row to keep, keep the whole group, or separate an individual record from a larger group. It also shows values that would not carry over.

The result is a reviewed CSV and an audit report containing the original candidate rows. Fields aren't combined, and nothing changes inside HubSpot.

I'm looking for feedback from consultants using exports they are authorized to process. If you try it, tell me one thing it matched incorrectly, missed, or made hard to review. Please keep the CSV and audit on your device; don't email customer data.

https://dedupesafe.vercel.app/for/consultants/community/

Reply in this thread with feedback using invented examples; please don't share customer data. You can also email dedupesafe@outlook.com. It's currently free to use. I'm the developer; DedupeSafe is independent of HubSpot.

## Own-portal invitation

Title: A CSV review tool for duplicate contact candidates

I built DedupeSafe to help inspect contact exports from your own HubSpot account.

Open a CSV in the checker, confirm its columns, and compare the suggested groups. Nothing is removed from the output until you approve a group and confirm the final export. You can choose which row to keep or leave the records separate.

The file is processed on your device. No DedupeSafe account or HubSpot connection is required. The output doesn't merge fields or update contacts in your portal.

## FAQ replies

- Does it clean my HubSpot portal? It creates a reviewed CSV. It doesn't change, delete or merge records in HubSpot.
- Are fields combined? No. The selected source row is kept. Review the differing values and save the original and audit.
- Can it be wrong? Yes. The groups are suggestions; the score isn't a probability. Keep records separate when unsure.
- What if it misses a duplicate? Report the pattern using invented data. No general accuracy percentage has been established.
- Does my file upload? The checker processes it locally and has no analytics. Marketing pages use pageview analytics. Email feedback is a separate, voluntary transmission.
- Price? Currently free to use; no permanent pricing promise.
- Support? Reply in the original discussion, use the public bug tracker, or email dedupesafe@outlook.com; don't send CSVs, audits or customer personal data.
