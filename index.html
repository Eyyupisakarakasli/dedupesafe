All limitations ↗
Your file stays local
The checker processes your CSV in your browser and runs without analytics. Privacy details
Keep a record of your decisions
The audit includes original values from candidate rows. The reviewed CSV keeps the selected row; it does not combine fields.
Your CRM stays unchanged
DedupeSafe does not connect to HubSpot. Reimporting a smaller CSV does not merge or delete existing CRM records.
